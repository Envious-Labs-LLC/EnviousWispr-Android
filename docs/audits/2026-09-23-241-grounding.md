## A. The warm hold today

After an eligible earbud take, the capture thread starts a 30-second hold under `sessionLock`. It carries the communication route, plays silence on an `AudioTrack`, and gives `AudioCaptureService` a started lifetime so it can outlive the take owner’s unbind ([AudioCaptureService.kt:872](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/AudioCaptureService.kt:872), [WarmHoldOwner.kt:73](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/WarmHoldOwner.kt:73), [WarmHoldOwner.kt:133](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/WarmHoldOwner.kt:133)). The silence writes run on `WarmHoldSilence`; the expiry and device callbacks run on `AudioRouteThread`. A new take adopts the route under the session lock. Service destruction closes the hold on the service thread ([WarmHoldOwner.kt:89](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/WarmHoldOwner.kt:89), [AudioCaptureService.kt:218](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/AudioCaptureService.kt:218), [AudioCaptureService.kt:460](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/AudioCaptureService.kt:460), [AudioCaptureService.kt:963](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/AudioCaptureService.kt:963)).

The lock keeps hold identity, route transfer, expiry, listeners, and service-lifetime decisions in order. An ordinary end stops the track, releases the route, removes the expiry and listeners, then calls `stopSelf()` if no session remains. Handover stops the track but transfers the route and keeps the service for the new take ([WarmHold.kt:43](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/WarmHold.kt:43), [WarmHoldOwner.kt:112](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/WarmHoldOwner.kt:112)).

## B. AudioTrackSilence

Its thread repeatedly writes 100 ms of zeros. A negative result breaks the loop; a thrown write escapes the thread. Neither reports failure or releases the track, so the hold owner still considers playback active until another end event ([WarmHoldOwner.kt:165](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/WarmHoldOwner.kt:165), [WarmHoldOwner.kt:197](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/WarmHoldOwner.kt:197)).

The measured warm-route benefit depends on *active playback*. After failure, the earbud mic route may therefore go cold and the next take may start slowly or deaf, while route ownership and the started service remain until expiry. There is potential needless battery use. A foreground promotion or hold notification is **NOT VERIFIED**: this service uses `startService`, with no `startForeground` call in the inspected file ([bluetooth-capture-android.md:95](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/.claude/knowledge/bluetooth-capture-android.md:95), [AudioCaptureService.kt:229](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/AudioCaptureService.kt:229)).

## C. End paths

The six existing reason tokens are `expired`, `device-changed`, `device-removed`, `new-take`, `destroyed`, and **`track-failed`**. The last already covers failed playback startup and failed service keepalive; no new token is needed. These are string constants, not an enum. No exhaustive `when` over hold reasons appears in the inspected files. The separate exhaustive `when` over *take endings* controls whether a hold may start ([WarmHold.kt:63](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/WarmHold.kt:63), [WarmHoldOwner.kt:60](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/WarmHoldOwner.kt:60), [WarmHoldOwner.kt:138](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/audio/WarmHoldOwner.kt:138)).

## D. Recommended design

- Give each new silence track a one-shot `onWriteFailed` callback.
- On a negative or thrown write, signal it only if `stop()` was not intentional; do not end the hold on the write thread.
- Post the signal through the injected route scheduler to `AudioRouteThread`.
- Under `sessionLock`, require `warmHold === failedHold`, then call `failedHold.end(END_TRACK_FAILED)`. A late signal cannot end an adopted or replacement hold.
- Reuse normal cleanup and the existing `route hold end=track-failed` log. Record only a bounded failure kind or code; remote telemetry delivery is **NOT VERIFIED** in the permitted files.

## F. Risks

- `stop()` can itself unblock `write()` with an error; suppress that expected callback.
- A failure can arrive while `start()` is still registering expiry and listeners; the posted, locked end must run after registration.
- A zero-byte write could spin without triggering the specified negative-write rule.
- Notification behavior and remote telemetry remain **UNCHECKED** outside the allowed files.
