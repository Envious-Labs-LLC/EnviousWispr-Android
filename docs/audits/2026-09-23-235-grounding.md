## B. Why save precedes insertion

The stated reason is to keep insertion from outrunning the durable row that tracks its outcome ([HistoryPublicationPolicy.kt](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/.claude/worktrees/issue-234-polish-optional/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/history/HistoryPublicationPolicy.kt:3)). Inserting first would hand the paste service no saved row ID to report back to, could leave the take absent from History, and could leave a late `ready_for_insertion` row open. Startup recovery closes stale draft and ready rows ([TranscriptRepository.kt](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/.claude/worktrees/issue-234-polish-optional/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/history/TranscriptRepository.kt:64)); its exact user-facing behavior is **NOT VERIFIED** because the recovery implementation was outside the requested reads.

## C. Stall and failure paths

The queue has one worker and an unlimited channel. An earlier held write, a stalled Room call, or a database lock can hold every later save; a cancelled worker or refused enqueue can leave its result unanswered. A Room insert or finalize can also throw; the finalizer turns that into a failed result ([HistoryWriteQueue.kt](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/.claude/worktrees/issue-234-polish-optional/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/history/HistoryWriteQueue.kt:38), [SessionFinalizer.kt](/Users/m4pro_sv/Developer/EnviousLabs/EnviousWispr-Android/.claude/worktrees/issue-234-polish-optional/app/src/main/java/com/envi/wispr/ui/SessionFinalizer.kt:188)). ## E. Recommended design

- Give the save a **500 ms bound**. This is a proposed short-dictation latency limit, not a measured Room threshold; measure it on the founder phone before settling it.
- Return typed `Saved(id)`, `Failed(cause)`, or `TimedOut`. Let one take-scoped, atomic delivery claim choose the route once.
- On `Failed` or `TimedOut`, commit `COMPLETED`, copy the final text, announce the measured destination, and log the failure or timeout without transcript content.
- Keep the queued save alive. If it finishes late, it must never call insertion or announce again.
- Reconcile a late successful row to the **actual clipboard outcome**, rather than leaving `pending` or implying insertion. If persistence ultimately fails, History may remain missing; the clipboard is the immediate fallback.

## G. Risks

1. A clipboard write can fail too; the current message reports that loss.
2. A process death can prevent late row reconciliation.
3. A timeout racing a successful save needs the same first-wins decision.
4. An unreconciled late ready row could be mistaken by startup recovery for an insertion that was interrupted.
5. A 500 ms bound may send healthy but slow saves to copy-only; phone measurement should decide whether to adjust it.
