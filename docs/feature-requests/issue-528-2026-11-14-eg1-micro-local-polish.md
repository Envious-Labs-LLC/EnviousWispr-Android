## §0 Problem, Goal & Non-Goals

### Problem
`EnviousWispr` on macOS ships `EG-1 Micro` (`eg1-micro-1.0-g3d`), producing significantly better email formatting, punctuation repair, and proper-name preservation than `S1-mini`. Android V1 currently hardcodes a single on-phone polish model (`S1-mini`, single GGUF file, `S1Config`, S1 control-line prompt, `KEY_S1_PROCESSING`) in manifest, UI, Binder policy, `:polish` service, JNI, and telemetry observers.

### Goal
Wire `EG-1 Micro` (`eg1-micro-1.0-g3d-Q4_K_M`, split into two `< 300 MiB` GGUF shards hosted on `models.enviouslabs.co`) as a first-party selectable on-phone AI Polish model alongside `S1-mini` so Android users (starting on Google Play `internal-testing`) can download, select, polish with, monitor, and remove `EG-1 Micro` without regressing `S1-mini`.

### Non-Goals
- Do NOT replace `S1-mini` as default or download `EG-1 Micro` during first-run onboarding.
- Do NOT bundle `eg1-micro-dflash` speculative decoding (Stage 1 proved negative/marginal net latency on 4 CPU threads alongside `+570-1,050 MB` RAM and `+60-103 MB` download).
- Do NOT route `EG-1 Micro` through `S1GenieXRuntime` (`geniex_gpu` failed with `S1BackendLoadException` on Pixel 8 and `3/6` hallucinated/drifted outputs on Galaxy S24; CPU-only `:llama-android` scored `12/12` on both).
- Do NOT wire `EG-1 Micro` Q4_0 (failed Stage 1 fidelity `21/64` diffs with hallucinations/drops) or Q5_K_M (`+50.7 MB` over Q4_K_M, `> 300 MiB` per shard threshold unless 3-sharded).
- Do NOT invent calibration dot scores (`1-3` `Accuracy` dots) for EG-1 Micro.
