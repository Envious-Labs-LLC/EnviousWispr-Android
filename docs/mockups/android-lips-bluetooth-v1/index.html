When the blue shows. The lips turn blue as soon as the earbuds are the chosen microphone: with Input device on Auto and earbuds connected, or with the earbuds picked by name. They go back to the brand rainbow when the earbuds disconnect or you pick the phone. The rail, the outline and the glow follow the lips. The spin while the earbuds warm up keeps whichever colours the lips are showing.
